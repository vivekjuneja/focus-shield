How it works

A pause button with a conscience.

Blocking is easy. The hard moment is when you want to turn the blocker off. That's where Focus Shield does its real work.

  1. The shield is on by default.Open YouTube, Netflix, X, Instagram, LinkedIn or Prime Video in Chrome or Safari and you'll see a calm page instead.
  2. Want to pause it? First, three facts.One at a time, drawn from Jonathan Haidt, Johann Hari, Cal Newport, Gloria Mark and others. You never see the same one twice in a row.
  3. Each fact asks: are you sure?Keep focused with one click, or ask to be convinced further. You have to read each fact for a few seconds before you can continue.
  4. Still sure? Choose 30 minutes or an hour.When time is up, the shield switches itself back on. No need to remember.

Is it safe?

Straight answers, before you download.

You should be careful about what you install. Here is exactly what Focus Shield does, what it doesn't do, and what your Mac will show you along the way.

Checked by Apple before you open it

The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized. That means Apple scanned it for malicious software and confirmed who made it.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Focus Shield never connects to the internet. It doesn't record your browsing history, and there are no ads or tracking. The source code is public, so anyone can check.

No account, no password, no payment

It never asks for your email, your Apple ID or your Mac password. If anything claiming to be Focus Shield asks for these, don't enter them.

Easy to remove

Pause the shield, choose Quit from its menu, then drag Focus Shield from your Applications folder to the Trash. That's all.

The first time you open it

“Focus Shield” is an app downloaded from the Internet. Are you sure you want to open it? Apple checked it for malicious software and none was detected.
CancelOpen

This is normal for any app you download outside the App Store. The line “Apple checked it for malicious software and none was detected” is what you want to see. Click Open.

When it asks about your browser

“Focus Shield” wants access to control “Google Chrome”. Focus Shield checks the tabs open in Chrome and Safari so it can block YouTube, Netflix, X, Instagram, LinkedIn and Prime Video while the shield is on.
Don’t AllowAllow

The wording sounds serious, but here's all it means. Focus Shield looks at the web address of each open tab. If it's a blocked site, it swaps that tab to the calm “shield is on” page. It doesn't read your passwords, messages or anything else, and it doesn't save the addresses anywhere. Click Allow, once for Chrome and once for Safari.

Install in about a minute

Four steps. No technical skills needed.

  1. Download

    Click Download Focus Shield below. A file called FocusShield.dmg goes to your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Double-click the file. In the window that opens, drag the Focus Shield icon onto the Applications folder.

  3. Open it

    Open Applications and double-click Focus Shield, then click Open. A small shield appears at the top-right of your screen, next to the clock.

  4. Click Allow

    Open Chrome or Safari. When your Mac asks whether Focus Shield may control it, click Allow. You're done.

Questions

Things people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Can I still shop on Amazon?

Yes. Only Prime Video is blocked. Regular Amazon shopping pages work normally.

What if I need YouTube or LinkedIn for work?

Click the shield in your menu bar, choose “Pause the Shield…”, read the three facts, and pick 30 minutes or 1 hour. That's enough time to reply to a message or watch a tutorial. The shield turns itself back on afterwards. You can also switch it back on early.

Does it work in Firefox, Edge or Arc?

Not yet. Focus Shield currently works in Google Chrome and Safari.

Does it block anything on my iPhone?

No, it only runs on your Mac. On iPhone, you can use Screen Time in Settings to set limits.

I clicked “Don't Allow” by mistake. How do I fix it?

The shield in your menu bar will show a small “!”. Click it and choose “Grant Automation Access…”. That opens the right page in System Settings, where you can switch on Chrome and Safari under Focus Shield.

How do I remove it?

Pause the shield, click the shield icon and choose “Quit Focus Shield”. Then open your Applications folder and drag Focus Shield to the Trash.
